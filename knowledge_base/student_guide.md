# Student Services, Campus Facilities, and Resources Guide
*Disclaimer: This document is illustrative educational sample material created for campus assistant testing and demonstration.*

## 1. Computing Laboratories and Technical Facilities
- **Turing Computing Center (Main Engineering Building, 2nd Floor):**
  - **Operating Hours:**
    - Monday through Friday: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM
    - Saturday: 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
    - Sunday: Closed for maintenance
  - **Hardware Available:** 60 Linux workstations equipped with NVIDIA RTX 4090 GPUs, high-speed dual monitors, and pre-configured CUDA/Python AI environments.
  - **Specialized Lab Access:** Graduate and Capstone students can request 24/7 keycard access through the Computer Science Department Office (Room 102).

## 2. University Library and Study Rooms
- **Main Campus Library:**
  - Open Monday to Thursday 7:30 AM – Midnight; Friday 7:30 AM – 8:00 PM; Weekends 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM.
  - Quiet Study Zones are designated on the 3rd and 4th floors.
- **Collaborative Study Rooms:**
  - Rooms may be reserved online through `library.campus.edu/reserve`.
  - Bookings are allowed up to 7 days in advance for a maximum of 3 consecutive hours per group per day.
  - Each room is equipped with a 65-inch 4K collaborative display screen and whiteboard walls.

## 3. Academic Tutoring and Writing Centers
- **Peer Tutoring Center (Student Union, Room 115):**
  - Offers free drop-in and scheduled tutoring for computer science, mathematics, and statistics courses.
  - Tutoring Hours: Monday through Thursday, 1:00 PM – 7:00 PM; Friday 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM.
- **University Writing and Communication Center:**
  - Assistance with technical reports, capstone documentation, and scientific paper drafting.
  - Appointments can be booked at `writingcenter.campus.edu`.

## 4. Health, Wellness, and Counseling Services
- **Student Wellness & Counseling Center (Building C, Room 204):**
  - Provides confidential individual counseling, stress management workshops, and crisis support.
  - Regular Hours: Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM – 5:00 PM.
  - **24/7 Crisis & Mental Health Hotline:** (555) 0199 (free and confidential for all active students).
- **Campus Health Clinic:**
  - General outpatient medical services, immunization checks, and prescription management.
  - Walk-ins accepted from 9:00 AM – 11:30 AM daily.

## 5. Career Center and Professional Development
- **Location:** University Center, Suite 300
- **Services:** Resume critiques, technical interview preparation, mock whiteboard coding sessions, and bi-annual Campus Career Fairs.
- **Fall Career Fair Date:** October 14, 2026 (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM at the University Recreation Pavilion).
- **Advising Appointments:** Schedule via Handshake or email `career@campus.edu`.

## 6. Transportation and Campus Parking
- **Student Parking Permits:** Required for all university lots between 7:00 AM and 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Permits can be purchased via `parking.campus.edu`.
- **Campus Shuttle (Blue Line):** Circulates every 15 minutes between the Main Campus Quad, Turing Lab, and South Residential Village from 7:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily.
