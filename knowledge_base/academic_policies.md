# Academic Policies and Regulations Guide
*Disclaimer: This document is illustrative educational sample material created for campus assistant testing and demonstration.*

## 1. Attendance and Absence Policy
- **Minimum Requirement:** All enrolled students are expected to attend at least 80% of scheduled lectures and laboratory sessions.
- **Excused Absences:** Documented medical emergencies, official intercollegiate athletic participation, and bereavement qualify as excused absences. Supporting documentation must be submitted to the Academic Dean's Office within 5 business days of the absence.
- **Unexcused Absences:** Students whose unexcused absences exceed 20% of total class hours will receive a full letter-grade reduction on their final course grade or may be administratively withdrawn.

## 2. Late Assignment Submission Policy
- **Grace Period:** A 15-minute grace period applies to all electronic assignment submissions after the official deadline.
- **Penalty Deductions:**
  - Submissions up to 24 hours late incur a strict 10% penalty deduction from the maximum possible score.
  - Submissions between 24 and 48 hours late incur a 20% penalty deduction.
  - Submissions between 48 and 72 hours late incur a 30% penalty deduction.
  - **Cutoff:** Submissions received more than 72 hours after the deadline receive an automatic grade of zero (0).
- **Extension Requests:** Formal extension requests must be submitted in writing to the course instructor at least 48 hours prior to the original deadline, accompanied by valid justification.

## 3. Academic Integrity and Misconduct
- **General Principle:** All work submitted for evaluation must represent the student's individual effort unless explicitly assigned as group work.
- **Plagiarism:** Presenting another person's code, prose, or intellectual ideas without explicit citation constitutes plagiarism. The departmental similarity threshold on Turnitin or MOSS is strictly evaluated: any uncredited code similarity exceeding 15% triggers an immediate review.
- **Generative AI Policy:**
  - Generative AI tools (e.g., Gemini, ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot) may be utilized for exploratory brainstorming, conceptual clarification, and code debugging.
  - Students must include an "AI Usage Disclosure Statement" in their submission explaining which tools were used and for what purpose.
  - Using AI to generate complete assignment solutions, essays, or exam responses without attribution is considered severe academic misconduct.
- **Disciplinary Sanctions:**
  - First Offense: Zero grade on the assignment and a formal written reprimand filed in the student's academic record.
  - Second Offense: Automatic grade of 'F' for the course and referral to the University Disciplinary Committee for possible suspension.

## 4. Grade Appeals and Regrading Requests
- **Filing Window:** A student who believes an assignment or exam question was graded incorrectly must submit a formal regrading request within seven (7) calendar days of the grade publication.
- **Procedure:** The regrading appeal must be submitted through the department's online portal with a written rationale citing specific rubric criteria.
- **Scope of Review:** When an appeal is lodged, the instructor reserves the right to review the entire assessment, which may result in a higher, unchanged, or lower overall score.

## 5. Course Drop, Add, and Withdrawal Deadlines
- **Add/Drop Period:** Students may freely add or drop courses without academic record entry through the end of Week 2 (September 15, 2026).
- **Withdrawal Period (W Grade):** Students may withdraw from a course and receive a non-punitive 'W' mark on their transcript between Week 3 and the end of Week 10 (Friday, November 7, 2026 at 5:00 PM).
- **Late Withdrawal:** Withdrawals after Week 10 are permitted only for verified extenuating medical or personal emergencies approved by the Academic Appeals Board.
